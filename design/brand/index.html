SIX OS · brand

Hex Face

A hexagonal band that spirals inward like the numeral six, drawn as one stroke the way a line of code is written, with two eyes at its centre. They are Pip's eyes: the mark is the system looking back at you. Source: the SIX OS Logo canvas, direction D.

App icon sizes

At 32 px and under, use six-app-icon-small.svg: the band is heavier and the eyes bigger, so they survive the pixel grid.

Colour studies

Cobalt is the brand. The other seven are studies for themes, editions and wallpapers. Each pairs a mark colour with its own near-black ground, and the eyes can differ from the band, as in Ink on Paper.

Using the mark

Clear spaceKeep one band width (34 units in the 400-unit drawing, about 8 % of the mark) clear on every side.
Minimum size16 px for the mark, with the small drawing at 32 px and under. The lockup needs the wordmark at 24 px or more.
One colourThe band and eyes are one flat colour on a flat ground. No gradients on the mark, no outlines, no rotation. The app icon may carry the dock's shared light.
GroundsWhite on cobalt or night, cobalt on white or paper. On photos, put the mark on a cobalt tile.
The eyesThe eyes may blink (every 4.5–5 s, 6 % of the cycle) wherever the mark is large and alive: boot, About, onboarding. Never in the menu bar or dock.
WordmarkSIX in Share Tech Mono, a small OS at 36 % on the same baseline, and a block cursor that blinks once a second when the wordmark is live.

The boot screen only

Boot screen

Power-on frame, the eyes open, the band draws itself around them, SIX OS types out and glitches once, and a terminal loader fills while "bravo six going dark" types underneath. About six seconds; any key skips it.
