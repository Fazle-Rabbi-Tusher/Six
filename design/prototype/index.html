Soft Matter

A familiar Windows map rendered in three materials: clay you press, glass that floats, paper you work on.

Touch it
Clay

Buttons, toggles, tiles, app icons

Float itPlasma glass

Shelf, menus, popovers, search

Read it
Paper

Window content, documents, lists

  • 1
    Familiar map, fresh materials. Start bottom-left, controls top-right, Windows shortcuts. We change how it feels, not where things are.
  • 2
    Touch it, float it, read it. Every element is clay, glass or paper, picked by role.
  • 3
    One obvious next step. Each view has one primary action that looks like one.
  • 4
    Calm by default, alive on purpose. Motion explains where things came from and what is happening.
  • 5
    Everyone, every setting. Try Quick Settings: reduced transparency, motion and more contrast are designed states.

One accent, calm neutrals

Iris marks the primary action, selection and focus. Neutrals lean slightly violet. Ratios below are measured live against the current surface; switch themes in Quick Settings to see dark values.

Materials

Pick by role, never by taste

Clay objects are small and filled, so their colour carries contrast. Glass only floats over content and never drops below 78 % opacity. Paper is where text lives.

Rules

Do put text on glass only above a 78 % fill; turn on Reduce transparency and check it still works.
Don't show state with shadow alone. Toggled-on clay changes fill, glyph and label.
