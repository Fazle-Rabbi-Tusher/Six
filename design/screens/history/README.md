# October 2026 pictures

These 47 pictures are from the clickable prototype of Soft Matter (`../../prototype/index.html`),
taken between 12 and 21 October 2026. They are kept for the record. They show a design SIX no
longer has in several places: the assistant (then "Six", and "Pip" before that) as a big orb on
home and as an orb and capsule at the right of the menu bar instead of the notch; Control Center
as round buttons instead of a board of tiles; the cobalt boot animation; a wordmark with "OS"; and
the prototype's sample content (a person, meetings, files, a battery level), which is not SIX's.
The Sculpted design, SIX's default since 31 October 2026, is not in any of them.

For today's screens see [`../README.md`](../README.md).
